## Decision

**Production retrieval = structured chunks → hybrid (BM25 + Voyage `voyage-4` vectors, RRF) → Cohere `rerank-v4.0-pro`
→ "I don't know" gate at relevance 0.76.** Chosen by the project owner after the comparison above.

### Why structured chunking
- Recursive 1000/200 found the right *page* but the wrong *chunk* for "What is included in the anal cancer package?":
  the split separated "Included in the Out-Patient Program" from the words "anal cancer", so the three near-identical
  package templates competed. Structured chunks carry `[Treatment package: Alternative Anal Cancer Treatment | ITC |
  Tijuana, Mexico]` on every chunk and win (0.86–0.97 with any reranker).
- Atomic price chunks keep currency and page context together ("$18,995 USD (Price starting from)").

### Why Cohere rerank-v4.0-pro
- Same accuracy as Voyage rerank-3 on the PDF tests (26/27 each before the fix below) and the highest relevance
  scores for correct chunks (0.84–0.99).
- Moving the rerank off Voyage halves Voyage traffic: 1 Voyage call per question (query embedding) instead of 2, so
  the free tier (3 requests/min) serves ~3 questions/min instead of ~1.5. Cohere trial: 10 rerank calls/min,
  ~1,000/month — enough for an MVP and the review week.
- Pro's scores are compressed upward (booking question 0.61–0.68 because of the "Request a Consultation" chunk), so
